# Testing Tunton: a guide for testers

Thank you for testing Tunton, a multicab tracking app for Butuan City. Testing takes about 20 to 30 minutes, then a 10-minute survey.

## 1. Install the app

1. Open the download link we sent you on your Android phone. Tunton runs on Android only.
2. Download the APK file and open it.
3. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to "Install unknown apps", then go back and tap **Install**.
4. If Google Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. The app is not on the Play Store yet.

## 2. Sign up

1. Open Tunton and create an account with your email and a password.
2. Follow the short introduction: pick an avatar, your fare type (Regular, Student, Senior or PWD) and, if you like, your places (home, school or work).
3. When the app asks for location, allow it **While using the app**. The map, check-in and relay need it.

## 3. Live data and Demo data

- **Live data** (the default) uses real accounts and the real server. Real multicabs appear only when a driver phone is running nearby.
- **Demo data** shows simulated multicabs on every route, so you can try every feature anywhere. Turn it on in **Menu → Settings & preferences → Demo data**. Nothing you do in Demo data is sent to the server.
- If you are not riding a multicab with a driver phone, use Demo data for the relay and tracking tasks.

## 4. What to try

Tick these off as you go. Each one takes a minute or two.

**Map (first tab)**
1. Tap **Routes** and pick a route. Its line and stops appear.
2. Tap a multicab on the map and tap **Track**. You see its arrival time; tracking uses Energy.
3. Tap **Locate me** (the target button) to find yourself.
4. Tap **Ask Tunie** and ask, for example, "How do I get to Robinsons?" or "Fare from SM to Gaisano?".

**Check-in (second tab)**
5. Standing at a stop: pick **At a stop**, then check in. Drivers on that route are told someone is waiting.
6. Standing by a route road between stops: pick **Near the route** and check in.
7. Walk away from the stop: the check-in ends by itself.

**Relay (middle button)**
8. While riding a multicab (or with Demo data on, near a demo multicab): open **Relay**, pick the seats, your destination, and tap **Relay update**. The relay is sent once the multicab moves off with you.

**My Rides (fourth tab)**
9. Enter **From** and **To**. You get the route to take, the walk to and from it, and the fare for your fare type.

**Crowdsource (last tab)**
10. Read the reports from the last hour. Filter by route. Flag a report that looks wrong.

**Other**
11. Tap your avatar (or the Energy count) in the top bar to see your profile and Energy wallet.
12. Turn on airplane mode: the map, routes and fares still work, and the app says you are offline.
13. After 9 PM, the map switches to Night Mode.
14. Try **Menu → Settings & preferences**: dark mode, map labels, arrival alerts.

## 5. If something goes wrong

Note what you did, which screen, and what you expected. A screenshot helps. Put it in the survey's "What did not work" question, or send it to us directly.

## 6. The survey

When you are done, open **Menu → Give feedback** in the app, or use the survey link we sent you. It takes about 10 minutes.

## 7. Privacy

Tunton uses your location only to show you on the map, check you in, and share a multicab's position while you relay a ride. Relay positions are kept for 7 days (24 hours at night). You can delete your account in **Menu → Settings & preferences → Delete account**. Questions: emspar30@gmail.com.
