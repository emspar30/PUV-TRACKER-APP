- Expo SDK 57, Android only. No iOS code, config, scripts or dependencies.
- Pure logic goes in `src/shared` (no React, React Native or Expo imports). Every threshold goes in `src/shared/config`.

- One local commit per approved step. Push only when the owner asks.
- Never commit: `assets/data/route_14_directions.txt`, `assets/data/{BRAND,LAUNCH,TREND,VALUE}.txt`, `graphify-out/`, `scripts/__pycache__/`, and the saved tracker copy in `docs/` (`Tunton Build Tracker.html` and its `_files` folder).

- Chat replies are terse (caveman voice). Docs, commits and code comments are plain prose. Questions to the owner are full sentences.

## 4. Fixed today

- An offline check-in or tracking start showed the raw network error ("java.net…"); it now says the phone is offline (`src/shared/util/networkError.ts`).
- Check-in has no At a stop or Near the route choice: within 40 m of a stop it counts there, else within 40 m of a route line it counts near the nearest stop; the card names the spot ("Near Flying V"), has no X, wraps long details, and a one-route Energy balance can switch the pinged route (PRD change 37).
- A returning account was sent back to the welcome and the set-up after sign-in; the username check ran inside the auth library's sign-in event and waited on its lock.
- A different account signing in on the same phone inherited the last account's username, badges, rides and history; the phone now remembers the owner's id and runs Reset app for another account (`src/state/accountOwner.ts`). Data left from before this fix stays until Reset app.
- The offline banner took up to 35 s; `expo-network` now reports Android's network changes, so it shows in about a second. Back online reads Energy again (it showed a stale balance before).
- A relay says "Get ready to get off" once at 300 m from the destination; the introduction's Arrival alerts row no longer says "Coming later" (PRD change 38).
- PRD 10.1 and 14 said the Smart Queue gives up after 10 minutes; the code uses 30 (D12). The PRD now says 30 (change 39).
