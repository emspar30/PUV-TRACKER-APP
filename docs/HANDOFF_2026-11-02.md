## 5. The SMS issue the partner reported (2026-11-02)

The partner said check-ins at in-between stops never queue an SMS. Findings:

- The in-between (fill) stops were removed in CA3 (`b7e6fa0`). A commuter between stops now checks in "Near the route", is stored at the nearest real stop with `near = true` (`check_in_at`), and the worker reads `worker_demand_near` and sends v4 ("SMB:3+1"). The partner's merge `4a85257` took this over his own sub-row design.
- The migration is applied; the worker is up.
- One real bug was found and fixed in `06ccba3`: with two routes pinged, a near check-in stored the nearest stop for both routes even when that stop belongs to only one. The worker counts a route only at its own stops (`services/sms-worker/src/cycle.ts`, `!(row.stationCode in counts)`), so the other route's driver got nothing. Now only the pinged routes that stop there are sent (`pingedRoutesAtStop` in `src/shared/ui/routeVisibility.ts`).
- **Not proven yet on phones.** Still to do:
  1. In the Render dashboard, confirm the worker's latest deploy is `4a85257` or later and "Live". After pushing the 7 commits, it redeploys by itself.
  2. Both test phones need commuter and driver builds from the current `main`. An older commuter build still offers fill stops, and the worker drops those codes.
  3. Run the tracker's CA3 checks: `near-two@1`, `near-one@1`, `driver@2`.
- Separate from this: backlog 132 says the Render worker sends **text** SMS, not data, since 2026-10-25 (`SMS_PRIMARY_ENCODING`), so updates also land in the driver's Messages app. Check the worker's environment on Render.
